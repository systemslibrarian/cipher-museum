Evaluation Status

No solver accuracy has been measured for this report. Earlier human, automated-solver and LLM percentages were unsupported estimates, not benchmark results, and have been removed. Difficulty labels are curriculum metadata, not calibrated success probabilities.

The downloadable “blind” split is public and includes answers. It is not confidential held-out data. Records labeled historical include teaching adaptations; the legacy verified flag does not establish source authenticity. See dataset scope and errata.
