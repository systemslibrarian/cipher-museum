Limitations

The Detective relies on standard English text frequencies. It will be less accurate for:

  • Very short messages (under 20 characters).
  • Plaintext in languages other than English.
  • Texts heavily skewed with names, acronyms, or non-standard vocabulary.
  • Ciphers utilizing nulls or non-standard alphabets.
