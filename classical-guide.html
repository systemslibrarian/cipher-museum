Useful connections

  • Nomenclators combine letter substitutions with codes for words or names.
  • Four-square also encrypts pairs; a 2×2 Hill construction provides a different mathematical way to process two-letter blocks.
  • Mary Stuart’s recovered Castelnau letters and historical investigations distinguish a recovered reading from the evidence used to verify it.
  • Enigma, Diffie–Hellman and RSA take the story beyond hand ciphers. Follow the timeline.
