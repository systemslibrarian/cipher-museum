## 4 November: evidence and decisions

The public description, homepage summary, README and book-page metadata now make the book’s focus explicit: what recovered words establish, when corroboration is needed, and which decisions become possible. The description follows the approved draft, with Mary Stuart’s correspondence framing the selected investigations and each chapter offering an optional museum visit.

The stated distinction is this book’s selection of investigations, sustained attention to evidence and uncertainty, and specific chapter activities. It makes no claim to have introduced consequences or reader participation to cryptographic history.
