# Reading the Evidence: four guided exercises

Added 2 November 2026 at `lab/reading-the-evidence.html`. The four source credits on the page point to public publisher or research-archive pages. **No link to a private Google Drive ebook or uploaded paper appears on the page.** All messages, keys, ciphertexts, archival notes, and variant spellings are original teaching fixtures.

| Exercise | Public paper | What was adapted | What was not implemented |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Compare candidate keys | [Pettersson and Megyesi, *Matching Keys and Encrypted Manuscripts* (2019)](https://aclanthology.org/W19-6126/) §§1, 3–5 | Literal key matching versus a separate plaintext copy and weak folder metadata. | Their archival materials, historic-language model and automatic ranking. The copy is supplied, not authenticated. |
| Classify mixed passages | [Gambardella, Megyesi and Pettersson, *Identifying Cleartext in Historical Ciphers* (2022)](https://aclanthology.org/2022.lt4hala-1.1/) introduction and conclusion | Explicit difference between cleartext, decrypted plaintext and unknown; digits and code switching can mislead. | Their automatic detection and published F1; pre-chunked classroom exercise has no span-boundary measure. |
| Test transcription and spelling | [Bruton, Beloucif and Megyesi, *Bridging the Low Resource Gap* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.63317/2twwf4kj3cgk) introduction and dataset design; [publisher PDF](https://www.lrec-conf.org/proceedings/lrec2026/workshops/resourceful/pdf/2026.resourceful-1.2.pdf) | Controlled clean versus one substituted/deleted symbol, and vocabulary matching historical-style spelling. | HistCiph data, authors' homophonic key generator, historical corpora, realism validation or reported metrics. Only a transparent 26-key Caesar search with five chosen words. |
| Show repeated-symbol structure | [Kambhatla, Born and Sarkar, *Decipherment as Regression* (2023)](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-eacl.160/) §3.1 | The first-occurrence numbering definition on a user-editable symbol list. | Their Transformer, training data, key recovery and performance. |

All controls run locally. The key comparison shows incomplete versus complete mapping without treating completeness as probability. The cleartext activity retains unresolved material and labels its supplied chunks. The transcription activity searches all 26 keys, keeps tied scores, reports edit distance only after search, and exposes the exact scoring vocabulary. It neither silently modernizes a text nor asserts a real manuscript source. Recurrence numbering preserves equality under bijective renaming and responds to a changed occurrence; it does not itself decipher text.
